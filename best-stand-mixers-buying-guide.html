Best Stand Mixers: Do You Need One and Which to Buy

By the HomePicksHQ editorial team · Updated October 2, 2026

A stand mixer is a big counter appliance that makes baking easier, especially for bread dough, cookies, and whipped cream. It is also a significant purchase, so it helps to decide whether you will really use it. Here is what to consider.

Stand mixer or hand mixer?

If you bake occasionally and mostly make cake batter or cookies, a hand mixer is often enough and takes far less space. A stand mixer is worth it if you bake often, knead bread dough, or make large batches, because it frees your hands and handles heavy mixtures.

Tilt-head vs bowl-lift

Tilt-head mixers have a head that tilts back so you can add ingredients and swap attachments easily. They are common and convenient for most home baking. Bowl-lift mixers raise the bowl on arms, which gives extra stability and capacity, and they suit large batches and thick doughs.

Bowl size

Bowls are usually 4 to 7 quarts. A 4.5 to 5 quart bowl handles most home recipes. Choose a larger bowl if you regularly double batches or make lots of bread dough.

Attachments

Most come with a flat beater for batters, a dough hook for bread, and a wire whisk for cream and egg whites. An attachment hub on the front lets you add pasta makers, grinders, and other accessories. Check which attachments are compatible before you buy.

Build quality

Look for a sturdy base, metal gears, and a motor that does not strain on dough. A heavier mixer tends to stay put on the counter during heavy mixing. Make sure you have the space, since they are bulky and heavy.

Care

Wash the bowl and attachments after use, checking whether they are dishwasher safe. Wipe the body with a damp cloth. Scrape down the bowl during mixing for even results.

Quick buying checklist

  • Be honest about how often you bake
  • Tilt-head for convenience, bowl-lift for big batches
  • 4.5 to 5 quart bowl for most homes
  • Flat beater, dough hook, and whisk included
  • Attachment hub if you want extras
  • Counter or storage space

Frequently asked questions

Can I knead bread in a hand mixer?

Not usually. Most hand mixers are not designed for stiff dough.

What speed should I use for dough?

Follow the manufacturer's guidance, typically a low speed with the dough hook.

Will it fit under my cabinets?

Measure the height with the head raised.
