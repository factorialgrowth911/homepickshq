Plan your word

Count characters before you order. A name like EMMA needs four letters and eight AA batteries. Short words read best across a room: a name, an age, LOVE, NOEL, or two initials with the heart between them. If you will reuse them, pick letters that work for more than one event, such as the family initial or numbers for birthdays.

Size and placement

At just under 9 inches tall, these read well on a dessert table, mantel, bar cart or shelf, and in photos. They are too small to read from across a large hall, so for a big venue plan to place them near where people gather or take pictures. Use the hanging holes for a wall display, and keep them indoors.

Batteries and power

Each letter runs on two AA batteries with a draw of about 60 milliwatts, so a fresh set lasts a long time. Rechargeable AAs save money if you use them often. Remember that each letter has its own switch, so turn them all on a few minutes before guests arrive.

Great for Q4

They work for Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's: spell BOO, NOEL, JOY or the year. Because the letters are reusable, one set can cover birthdays, holidays and anniversaries all year.

Quick buying checklist

  • Count the letters and symbols you need
  • Two AA batteries per letter (not included)
  • Under 9 inches tall, so best for tables, shelves and photos
  • Pick reusable letters such as initials and numbers
  • Indoor use only
  • Turn each one on before guests arrive
